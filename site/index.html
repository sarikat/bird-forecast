        What this is

        Every spring and fall, songbirds migrating at night are drawn to lit buildings and die hitting windows. Most deaths happen on a few bad nights. This page forecasts those nights for McCormick Place on Chicago's lakefront, so buildings know when turning lights off matters most.

        How it works

        A negative binomial model trained on 2,648 nights of collision surveys at McCormick Place (2000–2016, Field Museum) with O'Hare weather. Each afternoon it runs on the National Weather Service forecast. On years it never saw, nights rated High or Extreme were 20% of nights but 57% of birds.
